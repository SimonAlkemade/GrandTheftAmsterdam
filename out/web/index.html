De Gouden Kat

Coffeeshop De Gouden Kat banks its takings on Monday morning. It is Sunday night, and the week's cash is in a sports bag behind the counter.

  1. Drive to the shop. Follow the purple route on the radar.
  2. Stop at the door and press E (or GRAB on a phone). Your partner goes in for the bag.
  3. The alarm calls the politie. Get far enough away that they lose you.
  4. Drop the bag at the lock-up garage.

Stopping with a politie car on top of you gets you busted. A wrecked car or a swim in the canal and you're wasted.
