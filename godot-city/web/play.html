Amsterdam on foot

285,000 buildings from open map data, with trams, GVB buses, cars, Cantas and bikes on the real streets, all of them on their springs, and sloepen moored in the canals, under sun, clouds and rain, through day and night. Your first job: deliver five parcels along the yellow route, then sell tulips from the Bloemenmarkt to the tourists. Or buy a pistol at the gun shop, the blue circle, and see how long you last against the police.
